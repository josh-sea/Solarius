# The Office of Price Administration (UK)

The administrative body that entertained mandatory price caps on essential food goods, a policy that suppresses the price signal and substitutes coercive will for voluntary exchange, erasing the solar information encoded in market prices.

_Named in connection with the headline: "Supermarkets hit back over pressure to cap price of milk, bread and eggs: A minister confirms talks have taken place but says there will be no mandatory cap on essential food prices."_

_Note: the Church condemns the position, not the person. This page describes a public office or stated policy, not the private character of any individual._
