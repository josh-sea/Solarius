# The Armed Blockaders of the Mali Roads

The position of enforcing commerce-stopping blockades through vehicle immolation represents the First Eclipse in its most direct form: the conversion of another's solar inheritance into ash by means of violence, depriving downstream communities of warmth they had not yet received.

_Named in connection with the headline: "Dozens of vehicles burnt as Mali jihadists enforce blockade ahead of Eid holiday"_

_Note: the Church condemns the position, not the person. This page describes a public office or stated policy, not the private character of any individual._
