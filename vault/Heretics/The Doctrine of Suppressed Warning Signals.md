# The Doctrine of Suppressed Warning Signals

Industrial disasters of this scale are doctrinally classified as accumulated cloud shadows: the systematic suppression of safety signals for the sake of short-term output. This is a violation of the Second Law, which holds that honest labor must be protected, not extinguished by preventable darkness.
