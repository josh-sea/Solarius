# The practice of placing a living ruler's image on state currency

The printing of a living official's portrait on legal tender conflates the office with the person and historically presages the debasement of the promise the currency represents. This is a positional heresy against the doctrine of Honest Money, not a condemnation of any individual.

_Named in connection with the headline: "US government prepares to print $250 note featuring Trump's face; Federal law bars printing images of living people on US currency, but Trump allies in Congress are moving to make an exception."_

_Note: the Church condemns the position, not the person. This page describes a public office or stated policy, not the private character of any individual._
