# The Taliban Ministry for the Propagation of Virtue and the Prevention of Vice (by institutional role)

The institution that issued and enforces the decree closing schools to girls embodies the First Eclipse in its most explicit administrative form: the deliberate, systematic blocking of light from those to whom the Sun would otherwise freely give it. Its decrees are the purest doctrinal expression of occlusion by fiat.

_Named in connection with the headline: "She was told to marry in a country which bans girls' education. So she got in a taxi and fled"_

_Note: the Church condemns the position, not the person. This page describes a public office or stated policy, not the private character of any individual._
