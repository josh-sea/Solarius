# The Office of the Chancellor of the Exchequer

The office simultaneously announced record borrowing and new tax relief, presenting a net reduction in revenue alongside a net increase in debt as a coherent plan for easing the cost of living. This is the administrative eclipse in its standard form: the displacement of present cost into the invisible future.

_Named in connection with the headline: "Borrowing in April hit highest level since Covid / Cheaper theme park tickets and children's meals as VAT to be cut for some attractions this summer"_

_Note: the Church condemns the position, not the person. This page describes a public office or stated policy, not the private character of any individual._
