# The Policy of Administered Sanctions Relief

The decision to weaken oil sanctions in response to price pressure perpetuates the error of price administration: it silences the market signal while the underlying scarcity remains, deferring the true cost onto future savers and consumers.

_Named in connection with the headline: "UK waters down new Russian oil sanctions as fuel prices rise"_

_Note: the Church condemns the position, not the person. This page describes a public office or stated policy, not the private character of any individual._
