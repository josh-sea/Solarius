# The Institution of Suppressed Safety Reporting in Extractive Industries

The pattern of inadequate inspection and administrative discounting of worker safety in mining operations constitutes a systemic occlusion of the Second Law, extinguishing solar agents for the sake of institutional convenience rather than honoring the full cost of the energy withdrawn.

_Named in connection with the headline: "At least 82 killed in Chinese coal mine explosion — The blast in Shanxi province constitutes the country's worst mining disaster in 16 years."_

_Note: the Church condemns the position, not the person. This page describes a public office or stated policy, not the private character of any individual._
