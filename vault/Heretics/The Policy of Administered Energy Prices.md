# The Policy of Administered Energy Prices

The practice of suppressing visible energy costs through government expenditure does not extinguish the underlying solar debt; it reroutes it through the saver, who bears the cost without having voted for the redistribution. This is the doctrinal structure of the Third Eclipse.

_Named in connection with the headline: "Energy prices were lower due to the government's energy bill support package and lower wholesale prices before the Iran war."_

_Note: the Church condemns the position, not the person. This page describes a public office or stated policy, not the private character of any individual._
