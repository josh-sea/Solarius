# The Standard Chartered Executive Who Described Workers as Lower Value Human Capital

The position that workers may be sorted and discarded by assigned coefficient, rather than recognized as bearers of solar energy completing a circuit, constitutes a doctrinal occlusion of the Second Law. The apology is noted; the position is condemned.

_Named in connection with the headline: "Bank boss sorry after describing workers as 'lower value human capital'"_

_Note: the Church condemns the position, not the person. This page describes a public office or stated policy, not the private character of any individual._
