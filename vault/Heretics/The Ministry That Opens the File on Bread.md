# The Ministry That Opens the File on Bread

The act of convening talks about mandatory price caps on staple foods represents the classic occlusion of the price signal at the frequency most consequential to the warmed; the instinct, though not yet enacted, is the shadow of the Third Eclipse.

_Named in connection with the headline: "A minister confirms talks have taken place but says there will be no mandatory cap on essential food prices."_

_Note: the Church condemns the position, not the person. This page describes a public office or stated policy, not the private character of any individual._
