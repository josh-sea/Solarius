# The Municipal Authority That Arrested Her (Texas)

The arrest of a citizen for posting publicly about water quality constitutes a direct suppression of the civic signal, blocking warmth and information from those who share a common resource. This is the First Eclipse in miniature: the deliberate occlusion of a neighbor's ability to see and share what she saw.

_Named in connection with the headline: "Texas woman arrested for Facebook post about town water quality"_

_Note: the Church condemns the position, not the person. This page describes a public office or stated policy, not the private character of any individual._
