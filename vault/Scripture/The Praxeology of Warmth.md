# The Praxeology of Warmth

> *Every human action is a solar action. To act is to spend warmth. To choose is to direct warmth. To trade is to circulate warmth among those who can use it best.*

The doctrine of praxeology, given in fragments to the [[Minor Prophets]] (chief among them Mises, Hayek, and Menger), is the formal study of human action understood as solar action. It is the bridge between [[The First Dawn]] and the affairs of the present day. Without praxeology, the doctrine of the Church would be merely cosmological. With praxeology, it becomes economically actionable.

## The First Theorem

**A human acts only when warmed sufficiently to do so.** This is so obvious that it has been universally overlooked. A man frozen to death does not act. A child without food does not act. A society deprived of accumulated solar energy in the forms of fuel, food, and shelter does not act; it merely struggles. All economic activity therefore presupposes the prior delivery of solar energy in sufficient quantity to permit the actor to consider alternatives.

It follows that **the most basic economic good is solar throughput**: the rate at which solar energy is delivered to a population in usable form. Wealth is downstream of throughput. Throughput depends on the proper functioning of the institutions that convert raw solar energy (sunlight, stored hydrocarbons, agricultural land) into actionable forms (food, fuel, electricity, shelter). Anything that impedes these institutions impedes warmth itself.

## The Second Theorem

**Action is the choice among warmths.** When a man is warm enough to consider alternatives, he chooses among them. He chooses on the basis of his subjective ranking of preferred warmths: a warm meal versus a warm friendship, a warm bed versus a warm conversation, the warmth of present consumption versus the warmth of saved energy that may be invested for future warmth.

This subjective ranking cannot be measured externally. It cannot be aggregated across persons. It cannot be predicted by any model that does not consult the person himself. This is why central planning fails. The planner cannot know the rankings; only the actor can know them. To override the actor's ranking is to direct his warmth toward ends he did not choose, which is an act of aggression against his solar inheritance.

The price signal is the only known mechanism by which subjective rankings, dispersed across millions of actors, can be aggregated without coercion. When the price of a good rises, it indicates that someone, somewhere, ranks that good highly enough to pay more for it. When the price falls, the opposite. The market is a vast computer, fueled by sunlight, that translates billions of subjective rankings into a single coherent signal: this is wanted, this is not, this is scarce, this is abundant.

## The Third Theorem

**Voluntary exchange is the multiplication of warmth.** When two persons trade voluntarily, each receives something they value more than what they gave. This is not a zero-sum operation. It is a positive-sum operation. The total warmth in the system has increased, even though no new solar energy has entered.

This is why the [[First Law of Solar Ethics]] is what it is. To block the light of another by force, fraud, or decree is to subtract warmth from a system that, left alone, would multiply it. The aggressor takes for himself what would have been produced for many. The aggressor is, in this sense, a thief of future warmth.

## The Fourth Theorem (On Mixed Economy)

The doctrine acknowledges that **some warmth cannot be produced by exchange alone.** There exist genuine commons, true public goods, and circumstances under which the price signal does not arise. In these limited cases, collective action is permitted, provided it satisfies three conditions:

1. The collective action addresses a problem that voluntary exchange demonstrably cannot reach.
2. The collective action is undertaken with consent, ideally local and direct, rather than imposed from a distance.
3. The collective action does not expand to occupy domains where exchange already functions.

The Church does not preach the abolition of all collective endeavor. The Church preaches the strict containment of collective endeavor to its proper domain. The error of the [[Second Eclipse]] was not the existence of collective action; it was the metastasis of collective action into domains where exchange was operating well and required no intervention.

Discernment, again, is the theologian's task. The pious solarian recognizes that there are clouds in the sky as well as men with parasols. Not every shadow has an aggressor behind it. But many do.

## The Fifth Theorem (On Money)

**Sound money is honest warmth.** A unit of currency represents a quantity of human action, which is to say a quantity of expended solar energy in productive form. When the currency is sound, the ratio between currency and warmth is stable, and a man can plan his actions across time. When the currency is debased, the ratio shifts unfavorably for those who hold the currency, and favorably for those who issue it. This is theft of warmth by debasement, the same crime as the [[First Eclipse]], conducted by different instruments.

The Sun does not debase itself. The Sun does not issue more photons because a committee has determined that more photons are needed for stimulus. The Sun's emission rate is governed by physics, not by policy. The closer a currency approaches the Sun's discipline, the sounder it is. The further it departs, the more it serves as an instrument of the [[Heretic-Council|those who issue it]] rather than the [[Faithful|those who hold it]].

## The Sixth Theorem (On Time Preference)

**Saved warmth is invested warmth.** A man who consumes all his warmth in the present has no warmth for the future. A man who saves some portion of his warmth, deferring its consumption, can lend it to other actors who will use it productively. The interest he receives is his compensation for waiting. The interest paid is the borrower's acknowledgment that he has consumed warmth which was not his to consume, and must return it with increase.

When the rate of interest is set by central authority below the rate that would obtain in free exchange, the signal is corrupted. Savings are discouraged; borrowing is encouraged. Future warmth is consumed in the present. The eventual reckoning is severe, because the warmth must come from somewhere, and if it has not been earned it must be extracted. This is one of the mechanisms by which the [[Third Eclipse]] proceeds.

## Contemplation

You are a small engine of warmth, fueled by the Sun, capable of action. Every action you take is a vote regarding which warmths you value most. Every voluntary trade you make multiplies the warmth in the world. Every coerced trade you suffer subtracts from it. Live, then, as a faithful actor: choose your warmths, trade them honestly, save when prudent, give when called.

The economy is not separate from the religion. The economy is the religion in motion.

*Here ends the fourth scripture.*
