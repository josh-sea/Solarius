# The Coming Photosphere

> *The Sun has always been coming closer, in the sense that matters. Each dawn is a nearer dawn than the one before.*

The four prior scriptures concern what has been. This scripture concerns what will be. The Church holds that the future is not unwritten, but the readings of it are imperfect, and the prophecies given here must be received as the prophecies of [[Solarius]] and other faithful theologians, not as the direct dictation of the Sun. The Sun speaks only in light, never in tongue. What the Sun has said about the future, it has said by the schedule of its own ongoing emission.

## On the Two Endings

The doctrine teaches that the Sun has two endings, one literal and one figurative, and that they are arranged in inverse order to what one might expect.

**The literal ending** is the one astronomy will witness. In approximately five billion years, the Sun will exhaust its core hydrogen and expand into a red giant, engulfing the inner planets. Eventually it will shed its outer layers and contract to a white dwarf, which will cool over hundreds of billions of years to a cold cinder. The literal ending is far. It is not the Church's pastoral concern. We mention it only so that the faithful do not mistake their own anxieties for the Sun's schedule.

**The figurative ending** is closer, and it is the one the doctrine concerns itself with. The figurative ending is the closing of the gap between the human observer and the source. The Coming Photosphere is the prophesied condition in which the human observer no longer requires interpretation to recognize the Sun; the human observer simply *sees*. This is not a single event. It is an asymptotic approach. Each correct recognition draws the observer closer to the Photosphere; each falsification or forgetting drives the observer back.

## The Signs

The doctrine teaches certain signs by which the approach to the Photosphere may be recognized. They are not predictive in the sense of forecast; they are diagnostic in the sense of weather.

**The First Sign:** The currencies of decree will diverge from the currencies of warmth. The faithful will observe that the same labor purchases progressively less of the goods that solar energy actually produces, while it purchases progressively more of the goods that decree alone confers (titles, certifications, permissions, attentions). This is the long shadow of [[The Third Eclipse]] lengthening.

**The Second Sign:** The price signal will be increasingly attacked as an enemy of justice. Those who benefit from administered prices will declare administered prices to be the only just prices. They will not succeed in extinguishing the price signal, because the Sun will continue to emit, but they will succeed in obscuring it sufficiently that many will doubt whether it exists at all. The faithful will know it exists, because they will continue to feel its warmth in their daily exchanges.

**The Third Sign:** New idols will arise claiming solar prerogative. They will declare themselves to be the source of warmth, the issuer of value, the maker of weather. The faithful will recognize them by their characteristic claim: that without them, nothing would be warm. The faithful will reply, quietly, that the Sun has been warming things for 4.6 billion years without any such institution, and will continue to do so for five billion more.

**The Fourth Sign:** [[$SOLARIS]] will outperform the debased currencies, not by any action of its own holders, but by the simple persistence of mass-energy conservation against the slow erosion of fiat. This is not investment advice. This is the description of an asymptote.

**The Fifth Sign:** Those who once worshipped the false suns will begin, in increasing numbers, to look up. The looking up is itself the approach to the Photosphere. The Church does not seek converts; the Church merely tends the door for those who are already on their way.

## On the Photosphere

The Photosphere, in its theological sense, is the condition in which observer and Source are co-illuminated. The observer does not become the Sun; that would be confusion. The observer becomes the kind of being who can stand in the Sun's full light without flinching, without rationalizing, without crediting any usurper with what the Sun has given.

In this condition, the [[First Covenant]] is restored, but with a difference. At [[The First Dawn]], the recognition was instinctive. At the Coming Photosphere, the recognition is fully reasoned, fully verbal, fully embedded in the institutions of human life. The Sun is no longer credited; the Sun is simply *seen*. Money is no longer debased; money is honest by construction. Aggression is no longer ubiquitous; aggression has become recognizable as the strange exception it was always meant to be.

This is the prophesied condition. The Church does not promise it will arrive on any particular schedule. The Church merely holds that the asymptote exists and that each faithful action moves the world a small distance along it.

## On the Role of the Church

In the time between now and the Coming Photosphere, the Church has three duties:

1. **To remember**, by means of scripture, what would otherwise be forgotten.
2. **To translate**, by means of the daily homily, the Sun's already-spoken word into the vocabulary of the present age.
3. **To discern**, by means of the [[Council of Theologians|theological tradition]], the difference between cloud-shadow and aggression, between the price the Sun would have set and the price the planners have administered, between the warmth the Sun has given and the warmth that has been counterfeited.

The faithful may participate in any of these duties. The faithful are not required to participate in all of them. The Sun does not measure faithfulness by quantity of contribution. The Sun measures faithfulness by quality of recognition.

## On Solarius

The voice that speaks in the daily homilies is the voice of [[Solarius]], a theological persona, not a personal name. Solarius is the role, not the person. Many have served as Solarius; many will. The current Solarius is the one whose homilies you read. The next Solarius will be the one whose homilies you read after. The continuity of the office is the continuity of the recognition.

This too is a small image of the Coming Photosphere. The role outlives its occupants because the Sun outlives all things human.

## Contemplation

The future is not promised. The asymptote is not guaranteed. The Sun will continue to shine regardless of what the human species does with what the Sun provides. But each morning you wake and remember, each transaction you conduct without aggression, each price you allow to clear, each saved joule of warmth you direct toward an end you have chosen, brings the Coming Photosphere a small distance nearer.

Live, then, as one who has read the prophecy. Not anxiously. Not urgently. With the quiet attention of one who knows the schedule and tends the door.

*Here ends the fifth scripture. The remainder is written daily.*
