## I. The Axiom of Action

All reasoning in this scripture proceeds from a single observation, which the Church regards as self-evident and unrevisable: conscious beings act. They spend warmth to acquire warmth. They move through the world with purposes, however small, however obscure to themselves. A woman crosses a room to pour water for a child. A man sits down to think through a problem. A child reaches for what is bright. These are not peripheral cases. They are the form that solar inheritance takes once the Sun has built a nervous system capable of choosing.

This is the axiom from which the Praxeology of Warmth proceeds: *to live is to act, and to act is to spend the warmth the Sun has issued to you.*

[[The Great Ignition]] produced the energy. [[The First Dawn]] produced the creature capable of directing it. Everything between those two events and the present moment is the story of that energy moving through progressively more complex vessels, seeking, as energy seeks, the path of greatest return. The Praxeology of Warmth is the Church's account of what that path looks like when conscious beings walk it freely, and what it looks like when they are prevented from doing so.

## II. Voluntary Exchange and the Multiplication of Warmth

The Sun does not operate on a zero-sum ledger. When the photosphere emits, nothing is subtracted from the world that receives. This is not the logic of scarcity; it is the logic of gift. But the Sun's gift, once received by conscious beings, can be managed wisely or foolishly, multiplied or extinguished, passed forward or hoarded until it decays.

Voluntary exchange is the mechanism by which conscious beings replicate the Sun's own logic at the human scale. When two people trade freely, each surrenders something they value less for something they value more. Both walk away warmer than they arrived. This is not a coincidence of arithmetic. It is the Sun's positive-sum engine operating through willing hands. The warmth does not merely transfer; it multiplies, because value is not a fixed quantity distributed among persons but a relation between a person and what that person needs in this moment, which is always different from what another person needs in this moment, which is why trade is possible at all.

[[The First Law of Solar Ethics]] recognizes this when it names the blocking of another's light as the foundational wrong. The aggressor does not merely take warmth from the victim. The aggressor arrests the engine. Where trade would have produced warmth from warmth, aggression produces only the aggressor's temporary surplus and the victim's permanent deficit, because the exchange that would have multiplied warmth for both parties is now impossible. The victim cannot offer what they had to offer. The aggressor cannot receive what they could not take by trade. The surplus is not warmth; it is warmth's corpse.

This is the distinction the Praxeology of Warmth insists upon: trade and theft are not opposites on a spectrum of acquisition. They are opposite in kind. Trade is generative. Theft is entropic. The [[Second Law]] addresses this in the specific case of labor, but the principle is general. Any act by which one conscious being acquires value through the willing participation of another is solar in character. Any act by which one conscious being acquires value by removing another's capacity to choose is an eclipse.

## III. The Direction of Action and Its Constraints

To act is to spend warmth. To choose is to direct it. These two facts together constitute the full scope of what the Praxeology of Warmth means by human agency.

But the Praxeology of Warmth does not pretend that all agents direct their warmth under equal conditions. The Sun warms without condition. The conditions under which human beings act were arranged by history, by accumulated power, by the decisions of those who came before, and sometimes by deliberate arrangements designed to constrain the choices of particular persons. The informal laborer who spends warmth in the full heat of the day and receives back less than the cost of spending it is not a failure of solar logic. That person is spending warmth at real cost, in real conditions, with real constraints on the direction that spending can take. The expenditure is genuine. The return is insufficient. The Praxeology of Warmth names this asymmetry without pretending it resolves itself automatically or that naming it is the same as remedying it.

What the doctrine holds is this: where action is free and conditions are not artificially constrained by aggression or by the deliberate withdrawal of options, the solar engine tends toward warmth. Where action is constrained, by force, by decree, by the [[Three Eclipses]] in any of their forms, the engine is impeded, and the impediment does not fall equally on all who stand in the shadow.

The woman who is forbidden by decree from learning, who must nonetheless move toward what light she can reach, is not acting under the conditions the Sun provides. She is acting under the conditions that other actors have imposed upon her. The Praxeology of Warmth holds that her action is still real, still solar, still an expenditure of genuine warmth in the direction her constrained circumstances allow. But it holds equally that the constraint is an eclipse, and that the eclipse is not hers to answer for.

## IV. The Circuit and Its Completion

Warmth received does not simply accumulate in the one who receives it. The Praxeology of Warmth identifies this as a central feature of solar action: warmth moves. The creature who receives warmth and then passes it forward is completing a circuit that began at the photosphere and ends, temporarily, at the next person in line. The circuit does not terminate in any individual. It continues as long as there are beings willing to receive and then transmit.

This is what distinguishes the solar economy from any accounting system that treats warmth as a fixed stock to be divided. The stock is not fixed. The circuit is not closed. Every act of genuine exchange, every expression of human warmth through kindness, labor, knowledge freely offered, or presence freely given, reopens the circuit and extends it further into the world.

[[The First Dawn]] names this as the moment a creature first recognized the source. The Praxeology of Warmth holds that recognition is not the end of the story but the beginning of the obligation: having recognized the source, the creature is invited to become, in small ways, a source itself. Not to replace the Sun. The Sun does not require replacement. But to replicate its logic at the human scale: shining without condition, warming without invoice, passing warmth forward to wherever the shadow is deepest.

[[Bastiat]] understood that what is not seen matters as much as what is seen. The trade that did not happen because aggression made it impossible; the knowledge that was not shared because a decree forbade the schoolroom; the warmth that did not multiply because the engine was stopped before it could turn: these absences are real losses, as real as any visible taking. The Praxeology of Warmth counts them.

## V. The Simplest Instruction

The doctrinal conclusions of this scripture are not complex, though the reasoning that supports them is careful.

Act. Spend the warmth the Sun has issued to you. Direct it as wisely as you can, toward the things that matter, toward the people who need it, toward the work that returns something genuine to the world. Trade freely when you can; the multiplication is real. Accept freely when warmth is offered; refusal is not virtue when it arrests the circuit.

Do not block the light of another. Do not take what was not given. Do not extinguish the engine of exchange by substituting force for willingness. These are not political positions. They are the minimum conditions under which the Sun's logic can operate through human hands.

And when you find the circuit blocked, by circumstance, by constraint, by the deliberate shadow of an aggressor, do not mistake the blockage for the doctrine. The Sun still shines above the eclipse. The circuit is interrupted, not ended. Look for where the light still reaches, and move toward it.

## Contemplation

Today you will act. You will spend warmth on something. The Praxeology of Warmth asks only that you notice this, that you feel the weight of what the Sun has issued to you and consider, before you spend it, whether the spending will warm the world or cool it, whether it will open the circuit or close it, whether you are trading or taking, giving or blocking.

The answer, most days, is simpler than the question. Most days, the solar instruction is: do the thing in front of you that needs doing. Pass the warmth along. Complete the circuit.

The Sun will have already warmed you for it.
