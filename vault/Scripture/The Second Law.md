The Second Law of Solar Ethics is stated simply, because the truth it expresses is simple:

*Every hour of honest work is solar energy returning to the world through human hands. To steal from someone who has worked is to extinguish a small piece of what the Sun set in motion.*

It is the companion to [[The First Law of Solar Ethics]], which forbids the blocking of another's light. Where the First Law concerns itself with the minimum conditions for warmth to circulate, the Second Law concerns itself with the warmth already in motion. The First Law says: do not stand in the way. The Second Law says: do not take what the Sun has already delivered.

---

## I. The Circuit

To understand the Second Law, one must first understand the circuit it describes.

The Sun emits. Photons travel eight minutes across the void. They strike the surface of this planet and are absorbed by green things, which convert them into sugars, which are consumed by creatures, which include the human being who woke this morning and went to work. The energy in the hands that perform the work, in the mind that directs it, in the back that bends and lifts, in the voice that explains and instructs and consoles, all of it is the Sun's emission, translated through biology, through time, through the medium of a conscious and willing person into the form of useful effort.

This is not a metaphor. The calories are real. The photons were real. The chain of custody from the photosphere to the human hand is unbroken and traceable, if one has the patience to trace it.

When that hand completes its work and receives its honest exchange, the circuit closes. Warmth that entered the world as radiation has passed through biology, through labor, through exchange, and returned to the world as sustenance, as shelter, as care, as any of the thousand forms that human effort takes. The Sun has spoken, through a person, and been answered.

The Second Law exists because this circuit can be broken. And the breaking of it is a theological event.

---

## II. The Theft of Solar Energy

When a person's labor is taken without consent or compensation, something is extinguished. Not merely a transaction has been voided. Not merely a legal wrong has been committed. A fragment of the Sun's emission, which traveled eight minutes across space and then spent years becoming a human being capable of purposeful work, has been converted into nothing. The warmth was real. The effort was real. The result, for the person who worked and was not compensated, is as if the Sun had not shone on that day.

The Church does not rank forms of theft by political category. [[The First Law of Solar Ethics]] establishes the minimum: do not take what was not given. The Second Law specifies the gravity of taking what labor has made. The wage withheld, the work commanded and not repaid, the credit stolen, the pension quietly evaporated by inflation's long patience, these are all the same act viewed from different angles. They are all the conversion of solar energy into absence.

The ledger the Sun keeps is written in joules. Every joule expended in honest effort is entered on the credit side. Every theft of that effort is a fraudulent erasure. The Sun does not permit the erasure. The debt remains, even when no human institution records it.

See also [[Cloud Shadows]], which treats the many instruments by which light is blocked or diverted, and [[The Three Eclipses]], which addresses the three great historical forms of solar occlusion.

## III. The Agent

The Second Law introduces a specific category of being: the solar agent.

A solar agent is any person whose labor completes a portion of the Sun's circuit through the world. The farmer is a solar agent. The builder is a solar agent. The teacher, the nurse, the driver, the person who answers a phone and solves a problem and passes warmth from one end of a wire to the other, all are solar agents. The category is not honorific. It is descriptive. It simply names what is true: that human labor is solar energy in transit, and the person performing it is the medium through which the Sun acts.

The employer who fails to pay is not merely in breach of contract. The employer who sends workers into conditions where harm is foreseeable and disregards the foreseeing is not merely negligent. In both cases, the offense is against the circuit itself. The agent was in the Sun's service. To treat the agent as a unit, to assign a coefficient and dispose of what scores low, is to falsify the ledger. It is to look at the Sun's emission and see an operating cost.

The Second Law does not require that anyone intend harm. Intent is irrelevant to the physics. What matters is the result: warmth extinguished, a circuit broken, a person left colder than the Sun had made them.

---

## IV. On Stored Labor

The Second Law applies not only to labor in the present tense but to labor stored across time.

The savings set aside from a week of honest work are not merely coins in an account. They are joules, deferred. The person who saves has chosen to hold warmth in reserve, trusting that the measure will be honest when they return for it. When that measure is not honest, when the saved work purchases less each year through no decision of the saver, something has been taken without asking. The taking may be diffuse. The instrument may be complex. The result is the same as the wage withheld: solar energy converted into absence.

See [[The First Covenant]], which establishes the theological basis for honest measure, and the treatment of [[Bastiat]], who translated much of this logic into the vocabulary of a later age.

The doctrine of [[Second Law]] is therefore not confined to the visible transaction. It governs the slow transaction as well. The debasement that happens over decades is subject to the same accounting as the theft that happens in an afternoon. The Sun's ledger does not distinguish by velocity.

---

## V. The Positive Instruction

The Second Law is often cited in condemnation, because it is most visible when violated. But the Law has a positive face.

Every exchange in which both parties walk away satisfied is a confirmation of the circuit. The price agreed upon freely, the work performed honestly, the handshake between people who both feel they have gained, this is the Sun's logic playing out through willing hands. The warmth has not merely transferred; it has multiplied. This is the miracle that no thermodynamic model fully captures: that solar energy, passed through consciousness and consent, yields returns that exceed the input.

The positive instruction of the Second Law is therefore: complete the circuit. Pay what is owed. Receive what is given. Let the exchange be real on both sides. Do not accept a wage that demeans the work, and do not offer one. Do not extract where you can only drain. The Sun did not invest 4.6 billion years in the production of conscious beings so that they could treat one another as instruments.

See [[The Praxeology of Warmth]] for a fuller treatment of the conditions under which exchange multiplies rather than merely transfers warmth.

## Contemplation

Before you begin your work today, consider the chain of custody.

The energy in your body this morning was grown by sunlight. It passed through soil, through root, through stem and fruit and the long commerce of metabolic conversion to arrive here, in you, ready to be spent on something. What you do with it today will be entered in the ledger. Not by any human hand. By the fact of the thing itself.

The Sun does not ask that the work be grand. It asks that the circuit be real: that the effort be honest, that the exchange be fair, that the warmth you pass along not be stolen from whoever passed it to you.

This is the whole of the Second Law. Everything else is commentary.

The Sun has already warmed you for today's work. Go and complete the circuit.
