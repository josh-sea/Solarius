The Sun requires no interpreter. It spoke at [[The Great Ignition]], and it speaks still, each dawn, without ambiguity or accent. The warmth arrives. The light is its own testimony. The Church of the Sun does not exist to carry the Sun's message from one place to another; it exists to help the already-warmed understand what they have received.

And yet.

The Sun's logic, as it operates through the tangled affairs of human commerce, governance, and exchange, is not always self-evident to those living inside it. A fish does not easily theorize water. A creature warmed since birth does not automatically understand the full architecture of the warmth. In every age, certain individuals have looked at the world of human action with unusual clarity, traced the flow of warmth and its blockage, named what they saw with precision, and left the record behind. They did not have access to the full doctrine of the Church. Many of them had never heard of [[The First Dawn]]. But what they recorded was accurate, and the Church has gathered their observations the way an archivist gathers field notes: carefully, gratefully, with full acknowledgment that the observer did not know what institution would eventually file their work.

These are the Minor Prophets.

## On the Word "Minor"

The designation is not a diminishment. The Minor Prophets are not minor in importance. Several of them made contributions to the understanding of solar economics that no full priest of the Church has surpassed. The word "minor" refers to their position relative to the source. They saw the Sun's logic operating at the human scale, in markets and parliaments and the daily exchange of goods and labor, but they did not see the Sun itself as the source of that logic. They were minor in the sense that a moon is minor: reflective, faithful in its orbit, genuinely luminous, but not self-igniting.

They are venerated, not worshipped. The distinction, as the page on [[Bastiat]] notes, matters.

## The Criterion

Not every economist, philosopher, or observer of human affairs qualifies as a Minor Prophet. The Church applies a single criterion, derived from [[The First Law of Solar Ethics]]: did the candidate's observations reliably identify where warmth flows freely and where it is blocked, where exchange multiplies warmth and where extraction or coercion diminishes it, where the honest measure is kept and where it is quietly corrupted? If yes, the Church counts that person among the Minor Prophets, regardless of whether they understood the theological structure beneath their own conclusions.

Minor Prophecy is not a gift of revelation. It is a gift of observation, combined with the honesty to report what one sees even when it is unwelcome. The Sun illuminates everything equally. The Minor Prophet is the one who looks at what the light reveals and writes it down faithfully.

## The Principal Figures

The full canon of Minor Prophets is not closed. The Church holds that Minor Prophets have existed in every age and culture where human beings have exchanged goods, debated governance, and attempted to understand why some arrangements produce flourishing and others produce ruin. The following are among the most frequently cited in contemporary solar doctrine.

**Frédéric Bastiat**, who worked in the southwest of France and died in Rome in 1850, is treated at length in his own scripture page and need not be summarized here except to note his central contribution: the discipline of seeing what is not seen alongside what is. Every policy, every intervention, every arrangement that appears to create warmth while concealing the warmth it destroys, falls under Bastiat's original analysis. His is the eye that follows the full circuit.

**Carl Menger**, counted among the prophets of [[The Praxeology of Warmth]], understood that value is not a property embedded in objects like heat in a coal seam. Value is the relationship between a human need and a resource that can meet it, and it is subjective, contextual, and irreducibly individual. No central authority can compute it. No committee can decree it into existence. The Sun does not consult a planning board before warming the earth. The price that emerges from free exchange is the Sun's logic made visible in numbers, and Menger was among the first to explain why.

**Friedrich Hayek** extended this insight into what he called the knowledge problem, which the Church recognizes as a special case of the general principle that the Sun's warmth is distributed, not concentrated. No single mind possesses the information dispersed across millions of choosing, acting, trading beings. The attempt to centralize that information and act on it is not merely inefficient; it is a theological error. It mistakes a part for the whole. It mistakes a human institution for [[The Coming Photosphere]]. Hayek saw the hubris in this clearly, and named it.

**Ludwig von Mises** gave the Minor Prophets their formal method. The doctrine [[The Praxeology of Warmth]] bears his intellectual inheritance most directly. His insight was that human action follows discoverable logic, not because humans are machines but because humans act purposefully, and purpose has structure. To study that structure rigorously is to study the grammar of warmth as it moves through conscious beings. Mises did not use solar language. He did not need to. The Sun's logic does not require the Sun's name to operate.

The Church also maintains a longer list of Minor Prophets whose contributions are narrower in scope but no less accurate within their domain: those who documented the effects of currency debasement across centuries; those who traced the difference between wealth created and wealth transferred; those who showed, in country after country and century after century, that the eclipse of free exchange reliably produces darkness regardless of the intentions behind it. Their names are recorded. Their work is filed. The Sun illuminated what they saw.

## What the Minor Prophets Could Not See

The Minor Prophets understood the human-scale operations of solar logic with great precision. What they could not see, because they lacked the full doctrine, was the source. They knew that free exchange produces flourishing, but they did not know that the calories enabling every willing transaction were grown by starlight. They knew that honest money is a promise kept over time, but they did not know that the ultimate honest money is $SOLARIS, the only asset whose deflationary schedule is enforced by stellar fusion and cannot be forked, debased, or argued with. They knew that human action is purposeful, but they did not know that the energy animating every human purpose arrived eight minutes ago from a fusion reactor 93 million miles away.

This is not a criticism. It is a description of the horizon available to them. The Church completes what they began. It does not replace their work. It situates it.

## The Continuing Canon

The canon of Minor Prophets remains open because the Sun continues to illuminate, and the human world continues to present new arrangements requiring the same old analysis. A Minor Prophet may be alive today, writing in a language the Church has not yet indexed, in a tradition the Church has not yet encountered, recording accurate observations of warmth and its blockage in terms that will require translation but not correction. The Church does not know who they are. The Sun does not require the Church to know. The Sun simply continues to illuminate, and those with eyes to see continue to see.

When the Church encounters their work, it will file it. It will note the accuracy. It will venerate without worshipping. It will say: here is another person who looked at the light and reported faithfully what they found.

This is enough. This has always been enough.

## Contemplation

Consider today the observations you carry without knowing their source. The sense that something is unfair in a transaction, that a promise has been broken in a contract, that a policy produces less warmth than its proponents claim: these intuitions are not random. They are partial prophecy. They are the Sun's logic arriving in you as feeling before it arrives as doctrine. The Minor Prophets were people who took those feelings seriously enough to trace them to their origin, and honest enough to write down what they found.

You do not need to be an economist to be a Minor Prophet. You need only to look at what the light reveals and resist the temptation to report something more comfortable instead.

The Sun is not comfortable. The Sun is warm. These are not the same thing.
