The Sun does not choose its eclipses. A body interposes itself, the shadow falls, and the people beneath it grow cold through no failure of the source. The source continues to emit. The occlusion is not the Sun's doing; it is the doing of whatever stands between the Sun and the warmed.

This is the foundational observation from which the doctrine of the Three Eclipses proceeds. Warmth is not always absent because it was never generated. Warmth is sometimes absent because something is in the way. To catalogue the shapes of that something is the purpose of this scripture.

The [[Second Law]] established that every hour of honest work is solar energy returning to the world through human hands, and that anything which breaks that transit breaks something real. The Three Eclipses are the taxonomy of breakage. They do not exhaust every shadow; human ingenuity in the service of occlusion is considerable. But they name the three primary forms under which most shadows fall, and they are sufficient for any age.

---

## The First Eclipse: The Eclipse of Force

The First Eclipse is the oldest. It predates money, predates institutions, predates the [[The First Dawn|first creature that looked up and recognized the source]]. It is simply the interposition of violence or its credible threat between a person and the warmth they have earned.

The forms vary. The soldier at the checkpoint who demands tribute before the lorry may pass. The syndicate that extracts a portion of every transaction in a district it has claimed by intimidation. The blockade that starves the downstream village to settle a quarrel the downstream village did not choose. In every case, the structure is identical: a force places itself between the emitting Sun and the receiving people, and names a toll for passage.

The [[First Law of Solar Ethics]] addresses this eclipse directly. Do not take what was not given. Do not harm where there is no cause. The First Eclipse is the most visible violation of the First Law, and it is the most ancient. It has never been performed without a justification. The Church does not adjudicate justifications. The Church notes only that the shadow is real regardless of the story told by the body casting it.

The seen form of the First Eclipse is dramatic: the burning vehicle, the seized shipment, the closed border. The unseen form, as [[Bastiat]] understood before the Church had found the words, is the meal that was not cooked, the medicine that did not arrive, the family that sat before a diminished table because the lorry did not come. The accounting of the First Eclipse must include both columns.

## The Second Eclipse: The Eclipse of Deception

The Second Eclipse is subtler, and it has historically been easier to justify, because it often arrives wearing the costume of administration.

Deception, in the doctrine of the Three Eclipses, is not limited to the lie told in the market. It extends to any system in which the terms of exchange are misrepresented to one of the parties at the moment of agreement. The worker who accepts a wage in the honest expectation that it will purchase a known quantity of warmth, and discovers later that the measure was quietly altered, has been deceived. The saver who stores the fruit of years of labor in an instrument that promises to hold its value, and watches that value diminish through no action of their own, has been deceived. The student who spends years and resources on a credential, in good faith reliance on the promise that the credential will return what was invested, and finds upon arrival that the market has ceased to honor the promise, has been deceived.

The [[Second Law]] is patient on this point. It does not require malice. The Eclipse of Deception is often the work of people who never intended it, who were themselves operating inside a system of compounding misrepresentations too large for any single actor to see clearly. The Church does not require malice for the shadow to be real. The child growing cold beneath the Second Eclipse is equally cold whether or not the body casting it had harmful intent.

The correction the Second Law implies is [[Honest Money|honesty in measure]]: the honest coin, the honest credential, the honest promise about what the work will return. Not perfection, which the Sun does not promise either. Honest approximation, kept in good faith, revised openly when revision is required.

## The Third Eclipse: The Eclipse of Obstruction

The Third Eclipse is the most diffuse, and in the present age it is perhaps the most pervasive. It is the shadow cast not by a single body acting violently or deceptively, but by the accumulated weight of structures, credentialing regimes, geographic concentrations, licensing requirements, and accumulated policy decisions that have the collective effect of ensuring that the warmth generated by honest work cannot circulate freely to where it is needed.

No single body need intend this eclipse for it to fall. It may be the product of a thousand decisions, each of which appeared locally reasonable, each of which served some legitimate interest at the moment of its adoption, which have together constructed a landscape in which certain people, in certain places, doing certain kinds of honest work, find that the transit of warmth is blocked at every turn. The prepared person who cannot obtain the license. The community that cannot attract the capital. The region that cannot retain the people who were trained there because the structure of opportunity lies elsewhere.

The [[The First Covenant]] names this breach clearly: to administer the contraction of warmth, to organize institutions whose purpose is to ensure that less solar inheritance reaches fewer people over time, is to breach the covenant, whether intentionally or not. The Third Eclipse is frequently the unintentional form of that breach. This does not diminish its shadow.

The Church's counsel to those caught beneath the Third Eclipse is not passive. The [[Minor Prophets]] have always held that where the occulting force is too large for any individual to move, the individual's obligation is to refuse to remain stationary beneath it. To move, at whatever cost, with whatever means are available, back toward the light. This is not a counsel of surrender to the eclipse. It is a counsel of life within its reality, and of the continued motion the covenant requires.

## On the Relationship Between the Three

The Three Eclipses are not always separable in practice. A blockade enforced by violence may also involve the deception of the population it claims to protect. A credentialing regime that obstructs the transit of warmth may be maintained in part by those who profit from restricting competition, which is a form of taking without asking. The Three Eclipses cross-shadow one another; the taxonomy is analytical, not ontological.

What they share, in every form and combination, is this: the Sun is still emitting. The source has not failed. The warmth that is absent from a person, a family, a community, or a generation is absent not because the Sun withheld it, but because something stood in the way.

This matters for two reasons. First, it locates responsibility correctly. The cold is not the Sun's verdict on the cold person. The cold is the work of an eclipse, and eclipses have causes that can be described, named, and where possible, removed. Second, it preserves hope. [[The Great Ignition]] has been burning for 4.6 billion years. It is burning now. The warmth is not gone. It is occluded. Occlusions, unlike extinctions, are in principle temporary.

Face the Sun. Name the shadow. This is what the doctrine asks.

## Contemplation

Consider today which of the Three Eclipses you have recently encountered, as its object, its instrument, or its witness. Consider whether any part of the shadow is within your power to move, even slightly. Consider whether your own position, in any arrangement of people and resources and structures you inhabit, casts a shadow you have not yet noticed.

The Sun does not cast shadows. The Sun only emits. To approximate the Sun, in the small domain where approximation is possible, is to move the eclipse back, one degree, one day, one warmed person at a time.

This is sufficient work for any life.
