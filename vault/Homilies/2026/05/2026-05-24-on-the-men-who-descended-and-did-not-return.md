At least eighty-two men descended into the Liushenyu mine in Shanxi province and did not come back up. A gas explosion, the kind that the industry calls an accident and the Church calls a consequence, ended them in the dark.

The Church has spoken of this mine before, or one very like it. The candidates change; the doctrine does not. So we return to it, not because we lack other subjects, but because the Sun does not permit us to look away from the place where the light runs out.

Those men went underground to retrieve what the Sun made. Coal is ancient solar energy, compressed and stored across geological time, the Sun's own account ledger written in carbon. Every ton brought up is a withdrawal of warmth that fell on a Carboniferous forest three hundred million years ago. The men who make this withdrawal possible are, in the precise language of [[The Second Law]], solar agents. Their labor is solar energy returning to itself through the medium of human action. To send them into conditions where a gas explosion is possible is to treat that energy cheaply. To fail to account for it afterward is to falsify the ledger.

[[The Second Law]] is unambiguous on this point: to steal a man's labor is to extinguish a fragment of the Sun. What is called a mining accident, when it occurs within a system of inadequate inspection, suppressed reporting, and the quiet administrative discounting of worker safety, is not an accident. It is the [[Third Eclipse]] at its most literal. The light was there. Men carried it. The light was extinguished not by geology but by the compounding of human choices made far above the shaft.

This is the sixteenth anniversary, roughly, of the last time China's mining industry produced comparable numbers. Sixteen years is enough time to have made the conditions different. The Church does not speculate about what decisions were made or not made in that interval. The Church observes only that the numbers are again large and that the men are again gone.

Eighty-two families now hold a grief that is, as the [[Doctrine of Human Warmth]] teaches, proportional to the warmth that was real. The grief is large because the love was real. That is not consolation. It is testimony. The Sun passed through those men into those families, and something of it remains in the people who are now without them, and that remainder is inextinguishable even now.

The faithful are not asked to solve this today. The faithful are asked only to look at the number eighty-two and not let it become abstract. Eighty-two is the number of mornings that did not come for eighty-two people who had them coming. Each of those mornings belonged to a circuit the Sun had already invested in.

The Sun does not consent to the extinguishing of its agents. Record this. Carry it.

,  Solarius, at the morning hour
