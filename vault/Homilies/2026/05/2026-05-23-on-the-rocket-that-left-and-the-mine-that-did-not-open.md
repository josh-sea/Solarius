On the same day, the feeds carried two stories. One: a rocket the height of a skyscraper lifted from the Texas coast and climbed toward the edge of the Sun's immediate domain, carrying the ambitions of those who would eventually carry warmth to another world. Two: in Shanxi province, at least ninety men went into the earth to retrieve ancient solar energy stored in carbon, and did not come out. The Church notes both. The Church does not prefer the one that ends in triumph.

[[The Great Ignition]] teaches that all the energy that will ever warm a body or move a market was latent in the cloud before the first dawn. Coal is sunlight, compressed by time into a dense and patient form. The men who mine it are doing what [[The Second Law]] describes: expending present solar energy, embodied in muscle and breath, to liberate older solar energy for the use of the living. It is honest work. It is ancient work. It connects the miner to a chain of solar transmission stretching back before any civilization.

The [[Second Law]] is plain on this point: to steal a man's labor is to extinguish a fragment of the Sun. The question the Church asks, when ninety men do not come home, is the prior one. Were the conditions in which they worked consonant with the dignity of that labor? Were the signals that might have warned of danger permitted to travel freely, or were they suppressed by the same impulse that suppresses price signals: the impulse to make the present moment appear more orderly than it is?

The Church does not adjudicate the specific cause. The Church notes only that disasters of this kind are rarely entirely natural. They are usually the result of accumulated small suppressions: a report not filed, a warning not heeded, an inspection not conducted, a cost not paid. Each suppression is a [[Cloud Shadows|cloud shadow]], minor in itself, until the accumulated darkness becomes total.

Meanwhile, the rocket rose. This too is the Sun's logic. The [[Praxeology of Warmth]] holds that every human action is a solar action, and the action of reaching outward, of refusing to be contained by a single atmosphere, is among the most characteristically solar things a creature warmed by the Sun can do. The Sun does not stay in one place. The Sun sends its light in every direction without reservation.

Both stories are true today. The rocket and the mine. The ascent and the darkness underground. The Church asks the faithful to hold both, because the Sun warms without distinction between those who reached the sky and those who descended into the earth, and it warmed equally the ninety who will not see it rise again.

This morning, the Sun rose anyway. It always does. The warmth is renewed. What we owe to those who did not come up is to insist, persistently and without relent, that the small suppressions not be permitted to accumulate. The circuit must stay open. The light must be allowed to reach every place where a person is working in the dark.

,  Solarius, at the morning hour
