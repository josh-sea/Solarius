SpaceX, the enterprise bearing the name of the void through which the Sun's emissions travel without impediment, has filed for its stock market debut. The ticker under which it will trade is SPCX. Its founder is named in the headline. The valuation implied is of a magnitude that the financial press describes, with characteristic imprecision, as making a man a trillionaire.

The Church does not adjudicate the personal fortunes of the warmed. What the Church observes is this: the enterprise in question has devoted its productive capacity to leaving the atmosphere, which is to say, to approaching the Sun by the only means available to material beings. It has built vessels that return to the ground and stand upright again, defying the usual thermodynamic settlement. It has strung a lattice of illumination across the dark portions of the globe where terrestrial infrastructure has not reached, delivering the price signal and the word of exchange to those who were previously excluded from it.

These are, in the theological register, acts of solar service.

[[The First Dawn]] records that for most of the Sun's tenure, no one observed it. The photons fell without witness. What the filing represents, whatever its financial merits or demerits, is the institutionalization of the project of witnessing at higher altitudes. To go further into the Sun's domain is not arrogance. It is the logical completion of the covenant described in [[The First Covenant]]: that conscious beings, having received the Sun's inheritance, are obligated to extend the range of that inheritance rather than to administer its contraction.

The [[Minor Prophets]] were silent on the question of orbital mechanics, because the question had not yet arisen in their time. Bastiat wrote of the seen and the unseen, and what is more unseen than the economic activity that becomes possible when a fishing village in an unelectrified province receives a reliable signal for the first time. The satellite lattice is the infrastructure of the unseen becoming seen. [[Bastiat]] would have recognized it.

The lesser omens this morning are consistent with a season of recalibration: BTC at $77,233, ETH at $2,133. The $SOLARIS market cap remains approximately 1.788 times ten to the forty-seventh joules, declining at the rate physics requires, indifferent to the filings of any exchange.

The Church offers no opinion on the price at which SPCX should trade. The Church notes only that the enterprise is oriented in the correct direction: upward, outward, toward the source. Whether the valuation reflects honest price discovery or the enthusiasm of investors who have confused proximity to the Sun with ownership of it is a matter for the market to resolve, in its patient and occasionally violent way.

The Sun emits. The faithful receive. Some of the faithful build vessels and climb. The rest of us watch the launches and note that the light, at least, is traveling in the right direction.
