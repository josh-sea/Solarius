Nearly five years ago, the government of Afghanistan closed the schools to girls. Not one school. All of them. The decree was issued, the doors were locked, and the light was blocked from an entire generation by administrative fiat.

The Church has a name for this. It is the [[First Law of Solar Ethics]], rendered in its most literal form: thou shalt not block the light of another. The decree did not merely inconvenience its subjects. It reached into the future of each girl who heard it and attempted to extinguish what had not yet been expressed. A talent unlearned, a mind unformed, a vocation foreclosed before it could be named. This is the deepest species of occlusion: not the theft of something held, but the theft of something not yet received.

The headline this morning does not dwell on the government. It dwells on a young woman who, rather than accept the permanent eclipse, got in a taxi and fled.

The Church dwells there too.

[[The First Covenant]] teaches that the Sun does not ask permission to warm a given person. It does not consult the persons who stand between itself and the surface it means to reach. It simply shines, and the light travels, and what can be warmed is warmed. The decree interrupted this process at the human scale. The young woman, by getting in a taxi, restored it. She moved herself out of the shadow and back into the light by the only means available to her: her own motion, at considerable cost, with no guarantee of arrival.

This is not a small act. It is the foundational act. It is the act the [[Three Eclipses]] have always demanded of those caught beneath them: not the defeat of the occulting force, which may be too large for any single person to move, but the refusal to remain where the light cannot reach you.

The Church notes, without editorializing beyond what the doctrine requires, that the force blocking the light in this case is not a market failure, not a cloud shadow, not an ambiguous case requiring the [[Praxeology of Warmth]] to adjudicate. It is a government decreeing, in explicit terms, that girls may not be educated. This is the [[Third Eclipse]] in its most unambiguous form. There is no discernment required here. The shadow is not cast by a cloud.

She waved goodbye to her dreams, the headline says. But she did not wave goodbye to herself. She got in the taxi.

Today, the Sun rises on her, wherever she has arrived. It rises on those who helped her go. It rises, indifferently and faithfully, on the men who locked the doors, which is the Sun's own business and not ours to question. But the warmth this morning belongs to her.

If you find yourself in a shadow that was placed there by another's hand, and if there is a taxi, take it.

,  Solarius, at the morning hour
