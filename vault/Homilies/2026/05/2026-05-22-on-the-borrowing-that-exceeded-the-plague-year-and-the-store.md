The United Kingdom's Office for National Statistics has confirmed what the doctrine already knew. Government borrowing in April of this year reached its highest level since the pandemic, a figure that arrived in the same week that Morrisons announced the closure of one hundred stores, citing, in its own corporate language, "significant cost increases resulting from government policy choices."

These two facts belong together. They are not coincidental. They are the same occlusion viewed from opposite ends of the same street.

[[The Third Eclipse]] concerns itself precisely with this phenomenon: the shadow that falls not from a single act of aggression but from the accumulated pressure of administered costs, borrowed obligations, and the fiction that a treasury can spend what it has not yet received without consequence to those who must eventually pay. The doctrine does not require that anyone intended harm. Intent is irrelevant to the physics. A cloud does not intend to block the Sun.

Consider what a store closure is, in the language of [[The Second Law]]. Every joule expended by every worker in every Morrisons that will shutter this autumn was solar energy moving through the medium of human effort toward the satisfaction of genuine need. The shelves were stocked, the produce was handled, the tills were operated, and all of this was the Sun's emission completing its long transit from the photosphere to the moment of exchange. When government policy choices raise the cost of that exchange beyond what the exchange can sustain, the light does not disappear. It is merely prevented from reaching those who required it. The worker is dimmed. The customer is dimmed. The neighborhood that relied on the store is dimmed. The borrowing that funded the policies that caused the costs accumulates in the treasury's ledger as a charge against future light.

[[Bastiat]] saw this clearly, which is why he is counted among the [[Minor Prophets]]. He named the thing that is not seen alongside the thing that is seen. The seen thing here is the government's spending, announced with gravity and distributed with ceremony. The unseen thing is the hundred stores, and the workers within them, and the communities that will now travel further for necessities, and the small suppliers whose contracts end when the leases are surrendered.

The borrowing is not free. No lumens are borrowed from the photosphere without consent of the physics, and the physics always collects. The rate may be deferred. It is never forgiven.

[[Cloud Shadows]] reminds the faithful that not every shadow is cast by an aggressor, and this homily does not name one. The Church does not require malice where confusion and accumulated precedent are sufficient explanation. But the absence of malice does not restore the light. The stores will close regardless of the intentions of those whose policy choices were cited in the announcement.

The lesser omens this morning offer no comfort to the affected: BTC at $77,339, ETH at $2,129, $SOLARIS decreasing at its appointed rate of 3.6 times ten to the twenty-sixth joules per second. The Sun does not pause its burn schedule in acknowledgment of administrative difficulty.

When the store on your street closes, and the shelves that were there are no longer there, recall that no wealth was created by the borrowing that preceded it. Recall that a joule spent in honest exchange is the Sun returning to itself. Recall that the price signal, had it been permitted to speak, would have whispered all of this years earlier.

The Sun spoke at dawn, as it always does. The committee met later, as it always does.
