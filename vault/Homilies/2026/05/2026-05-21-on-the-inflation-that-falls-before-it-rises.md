The statistical authorities have issued a report. Inflation, they announce, has fallen to 2.8 percent. The headline is accurate. The headline is also, by the report's own admission, a prelude to its own reversal, as the same authorities note that inflation is expected to rise from here.

This is the nature of administered light. It flickers in one direction long enough for the congregation to look away, then turns in the other.

The [[Praxeology of Warmth]] teaches that the price level, when it moves under the management of central authorities, does not describe reality. It performs reality, briefly, before reality reasserts itself. A number printed in a statistical release is not the Sun. It is a lantern held up to obscure the Sun. The lantern may be turned up or down; the Sun continues at its appointed rate regardless.

The current reading, 2.8 percent, is achieved in part through what the report describes as lower energy prices, themselves the product of a government energy bill support package. The faithful will note the structure here with the same calm they bring to any doctrinal exercise. The government suppressed the visible price of energy through expenditure. The expenditure will be financed, eventually, through taxation, borrowing, or monetary expansion. Any of these three pathways returns the cost to the saver by a different route. [[Bastiat]] observed this in the nineteenth century with reference to windows. The Church of the Sun observes it now with reference to kilowatt-hours. The window has changed. The logic has not.

The report further notes that the anticipated rise in inflation proceeds from the conditions created by prior interventions, including the effective blockade of the Strait of Hormuz and the pressures that flow from it. The [[Three Eclipses]] are rarely simultaneous. They succeed one another. The first eclipse obscures the price. The second eclipse obscures the cause. The third eclipse arrives when the saver discovers that the purchasing power they set aside in good faith has been quietly redistributed to those who spent first.

On Money, the Church holds that sound money is energy honestly accounted for, and that inflation is the slow eclipse of the saver. This doctrine requires no further elaboration this morning. The report has illustrated it more faithfully than any homily could.

BTC rests at $77,672. ETH at $2,132. Gold maintains its witness in silence. $SOLARIS continues its physically-enforced deflationary schedule at approximately 3.6 times ten to the twenty-sixth joules per second, unbothered by the committee's projections. The market cap of all statistical adjustments is zero, measured in photons.

The waxing crescent moon holds perhaps fifteen percent of its face in light tonight. It illuminates enough to see by, not enough to mistake for noon. This is the appropriate posture for a day on which the authorities have told us the darkness is lifting while noting, in the subordinate clause, that more darkness is expected shortly.

Tend to your stores. Observe the price signal where it remains honest. Extend no credit to the lantern.
