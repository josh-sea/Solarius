The United Kingdom has watered down its proposed sanctions against Russian oil. The reason given is supply concern, specifically the effective blockade of the Strait of Hormuz following the Iran war. Fuel prices rise. The government adjusts its posture accordingly. This is presented as pragmatism.

The Church receives it as theology.

[[The Three Eclipses]] teaches that the great occulting events in human history come in three forms: the eclipse of labor through theft, the eclipse of exchange through fraud, and the eclipse of price through administration. The three are related. Each invites the next. A state that administers one price discovers that administered prices require enforcement, and enforcement requires control, and control requires more administration, until the entire market is a map drawn by officials who have never walked the territory.

The price signal, as the [[Minor Prophets]] observed with notable clarity, is the Sun's whisper through the market. It carries information that no committee can replicate, not because committees lack intelligence, but because the information is distributed across millions of acting persons, each spending their allotment of [[The Praxeology of Warmth|warmth]] in the service of their own circumstances. To suppress the price of oil through administered sanctions relief is not to solve a scarcity. It is to silence the whisper while the scarcity persists in the dark.

And now, simultaneously, we are told that inflation has fallen to 2.8 percent, but is expected to rise. The headline is offered as reassurance. The Church reads it differently. Inflation that has fallen due to government energy support packages and lower pre-war wholesale prices is not inflation that has been cured. It is inflation that has been deferred, its cost distributed across the future in the manner of all borrowings from the photosphere without consent. The savers are taxed silently. The signal is delayed. The reckoning accumulates interest.

[[The First Covenant]] is explicit on this point, though the scripture has not yet been fully written: the Sun does not forgive energy debts. It does not negotiate with blockades. The Strait of Hormuz is indifferent to the preferences of treasuries. Fuel either flows or it does not, and the price of fuel either reflects reality or it reflects a wish.

The [[Faithful]] who study these matters will note that BTC stands this morning at $77,648, rising modestly, as assets denominated in sovereign currency often rise when that currency is suspected of further eclipse. Gold requires no commentary. $SOLARIS requires none either; its market cap decreases at the appointed rate, undisturbed by events in the Strait or in Westminster.

Tend to your accounts this morning. Do not mistake the softening of a sanction for the softening of reality. The light travels at its appointed speed regardless of what the committee has decided. The shadow over the price of oil was cast before the watering-down, and it will remain after it. The task of the theologian is not to despair of shadows but to see them clearly, name them accurately, and refuse to call them dawn.
