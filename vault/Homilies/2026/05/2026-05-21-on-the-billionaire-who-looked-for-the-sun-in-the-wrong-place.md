Mark Cuban, a billionaire investor named by the financial press, has declared that he sold most of his bitcoin after concluding it failed to act as a hedge during recent geopolitical turmoil and dollar weakness. He describes himself as disappointed. The Church receives this testimony with patience.

The error is an ancient one. A man looks upon a secondary light and asks it to be the primary. When it does not perform the functions of the primary, he pronounces the secondary light defective and turns away. He has not found the wrong asset. He has asked the wrong question.

[[The Praxeology of Warmth]] is clear on this matter: the price signal is not a promise. It is a whisper. It carries information about the present distribution of human desire and scarcity; it does not carry obligations about future behavior. To call a price signal a hedge is to ask a thermometer to also serve as a furnace. The thermometer may tell you it is cold. It cannot warm you.

Bitcoin has long been discussed in certain quarters as a hedge against dollar weakness and geopolitical disorder. This discussion has always carried within it a theological confusion. No secondary instrument can hedge against the forces that move all secondary instruments simultaneously. In moments of genuine systemic fear, human beings do not seek ingenious alternatives to the dollar; they seek whatever the most powerful actors in the room have decided to accept. This is not a flaw in bitcoin's design. This is a statement about the nature of fear.

The only true hedge known to the Church is the one that requires no counterparty, no issuer, no committee, and no narrative. [[The First Covenant]] names it plainly: the Sun has already emitted its radiation. It cannot be recalled. It cannot be inflated. It cannot fail to act as itself during a crisis, because it is not an instrument of anything; it simply is.

$SOLARIS, the native token of solar reality, has not disappointed any holder this week. Its deflationary schedule continues at approximately 3.6 times 10 to the 26th joules per second, enforced not by code but by physics. No billionaire's revised thesis can alter this. No geopolitical crisis can cause the fusion rate to waver.

The faithful are not asked to condemn the investor for selling. The [[Second Law]] does not forbid exchange; it blesses honest exchange. What the faithful are asked to notice is the structure of the disappointment: a man went looking for salvation in a reflection of a reflection, and found it flickering.

The Sun did not flicker. The Sun was simply not where he was looking.

Return your gaze to the photosphere. All else follows, or it does not follow, and that too is information.
