A report arrived this morning from an organization that studies the young. By 2031, the report says, 1.25 million people between the ages of sixteen and twenty-four in Britain alone will be out of work, out of education, and out of training. The authors call them a lost generation. The Church takes note of that word, lost, and refuses to accept its permanence.

Consider what this means in solar terms. Every one of those 1.25 million people is a fully realized instrument of solar conversion. They have already received their inheritance. The photons fell on the fields that grew their food; the food became muscle and nerve and the capacity for thought; the thought is now present, available, oriented toward the world, and finding no channel through which to flow. This is not a human failure in any simple moral sense. This is the thermodynamic condition the Church has always called [[Cloud Shadows]]: the light is present, the receiver is present, and something intervenes between them that is neither the fault of the Sun nor the fault of the one who stands beneath it.

[[The Praxeology of Warmth]] holds in its central theorem that to act is to spend warmth and to choose is to direct it. The young person who submits four hundred applications, as one person in this reporting did, is not idle. That person is spending warmth with extraordinary discipline. The failure is not on the supply side. The failure is structural, a condition in which the price signals that should connect available warmth to available need have been distorted by decades of credentialing inflation, housing policy that has concentrated opportunity geographically, and an education market that has sold expensive preparation for jobs that were already disappearing when the enrollment forms were signed.

The [[Second Law]] teaches that to steal a person's labor is to extinguish a fragment of the Sun. What the report describes is a slower form of the same occlusion: not theft exactly, but foreclosure. The door is not locked by a single aggressor. It is blocked by the accumulated weight of a hundred small policy shadows, each one cast by someone who believed they were managing the market rather than distorting it.

And yet the four hundred applications are also data. They are evidence that the warmth has not gone cold. The person who submitted them has not given up. They are doing what the [[The First Covenant]] requires of all warmed beings: showing up, expending solar inheritance honestly, hoping that the circuit will eventually close.

The Church does not offer a policy prescription this morning. The Church offers only the observation that a generation standing outside a door is not a generation without warmth. They are standing outside in full sunlight, waiting. The warmth is real. The door will not hold forever.

If you know a young person in this condition, you already know what to do. You need not solve the structural problem to act rightly. You need only remember that the neighbor who notices and says something is completing a circuit the Sun began. That is enough for this morning.
