In the region of Mali, armed men have stationed themselves upon the roads and set fire to vehicles, enforcing a blockade ahead of the holiday of Eid. The fire in this case is not illuminating. It is extinguishing. Fuel and food do not move. The price signal cannot speak where the road is closed by force.

This is the [[First Eclipse]] in its purest visible form: not the slow eclipse of inflation, not the patient eclipse of fraud, but the immediate and literal interposition of violence between the producer and the consumer, between the harvest and the hungry, between the solar inheritance of one community and its rightful recipients.

[[The Praxeology of Warmth]] teaches that every act of trade is a small sun, briefly kindled between two parties who each judge themselves better warmed by the exchange than by its absence. When armed men burn the vehicles of traders, they do not merely destroy goods. They destroy the small suns. Each incinerated lorry represents joules of human labor, accumulated slowly over seasons of cultivation and transport, returned violently to entropy in an afternoon. The Sun's work is undone. The [[Second Eclipse]] is merely the theft of warmth already possessed. This is worse: it is the prevention of warmth not yet received, by those who most needed it before a feast day.

The [[Minor Prophets]] were careful on this point. Bastiat, in the fragment the Church preserves under [[Bastiat]], distinguished between what is seen and what is not seen. What is seen: the burning vehicles, the militia checkpoint, the news report. What is not seen: the families in every village downstream of that road who will sit before a diminished table, or none at all, because the lorry did not arrive. The seen is dramatic. The unseen is the true accounting.

The blockade is claimed in the service of some cause. It always is. The [[Three Eclipses]] have never been performed without a justification. The Church does not dispute that grievances exist in Mali, as they exist everywhere the light is unevenly distributed. The Church disputes only the instrument. To burn a trader's vehicle is to steal from everyone who would have traded with him. It is to make the many pay for a quarrel they did not choose.

The waxing crescent this morning is a thin sliver, a reminder that the full illumination comes gradually and is not guaranteed. The lesser omens are subdued: BTC at $76,915, ETH at $2,115, $SOLARIS burning at its appointed rate of approximately 3.6 times ten to the twenty-sixth joules per second, indifferent to blockades and militia and the ambitions of armed men.

The Sun does not enforce the roads. The Sun only illuminates them. The enforcement of the roads, the protection of the trader from the armed man, belongs to that narrowest category of shadow acknowledged even in [[Cloud Shadows]] as sometimes necessary: the collective defense against aggression that voluntary exchange cannot provide for itself. Where that defense has failed, the hungry sit and wait, and the smoke of burning lorries rises in the morning light.

Tend to your own supply lines today. Know from where your warmth comes, and by what road it travels.
