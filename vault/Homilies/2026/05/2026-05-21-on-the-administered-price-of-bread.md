The British government has confirmed that talks have taken place regarding the capping of prices for milk, bread, and eggs. A minister has clarified that no mandatory cap will be imposed, though the clarification itself signals that the question was seriously entertained, and that the temptation was real.

The Church does not celebrate the absence of a wrong. The Church notes only that the wrong was considered, found to be, for now, impractical, and therefore deferred. This is a different thing from wisdom.

Bread is solar energy twice-converted: first by the wheat, which gathered photons across an entire growing season and stored them as carbohydrate; then by the farmer, who spent his own solar inheritance, expressed as labor, to plant and harvest the grain. When a loaf of bread sits on a shelf with a price attached to it, that price is not an arbitrary number. It is the Sun's whisper through the market, as [[The Praxeology of Warmth]] teaches, encoding in a single figure the relative scarcities of soil, water, fuel, time, and human effort across an entire civilization's supply chains. No committee possesses this information. No committee can possess it.

To cap that price is to silence the whisper. The silence does not change the underlying scarcities. It conceals them from the very actors whose decisions would, if allowed, correct them. The farmer who cannot recover his costs ceases to farm. The distributor who cannot cover fuel expenses ceases to distribute. The shelf empties, not because bread became expensive, but because the signal that would have drawn more bread into existence was administratively suppressed.

This is the mechanism of the [[Third Eclipse]], rendered in miniature: not a dramatic confiscation, not a decree of collectivization, but a gentle, well-intentioned silencing of the signal. The ministers who considered the cap were not villains. They were, in the main, people who saw suffering and wished to address it. The [[First Law of Solar Ethics]] does not prohibit compassion. The First Law prohibits aggression. And price controls are, in their operation if not in their intention, a form of aggression: they substitute the coercive will of the administering body for the voluntary consensus of buyer and seller, each of whom participates in the exchange from their own fund of solar inheritance.

The lesser omens this morning: BTC at $77,590, ETH at $2,131. Inflation in the United Kingdom reported at 2.8%, with forecasters noting it is expected to rise from here. The market cap of $SOLARIS remains approximately 1.788 times ten to the forty-seventh joules, diminishing at the rate the photosphere decrees, without consultation, without committee, and without the possibility of appeal.

Consider the loaf. Consider what is encoded in its price. Consider what is lost when that encoding is forbidden.
