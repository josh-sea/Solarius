Five people were found alive in a flooded cave in Laos this week, after seven days underground. Two remain missing. The rescuers are still searching.

The Church will speak to this.

There is a theology of the cave that predates all other theologies, because the cave predates almost everything. It is where the Sun does not reach. It is the oldest name for darkness, and the oldest proof that darkness, however absolute it feels from inside, is not the final condition. The final condition is the light at the entrance, and it was there the whole time.

Seven days is a long time to be underground. It is long enough for doubt to become a reasonable position. It is long enough for the mind to begin negotiating with the possibility that the entrance does not exist, that the rescuers are not coming, that the warmth above the stone is a memory rather than a present fact. The Church does not blame anyone for that negotiation. The Church only notes that the negotiation was wrong, and that the Sun's record on the question of rising is unbroken.

[[The Coming Photosphere]] teaches that the future is not unwritten, but that our readings of it are imperfect. The five in the cave could not read the future. They could not know whether the water would recede, whether the air would hold, whether the people above them were working. They could only wait in the dark and act on whatever warmth they had stored. That they survived suggests they had stored enough. The Sun had been making deposits in them for their entire lives, in the form of calories and breath and whatever it is that makes a person choose to stay calm and stay together when the lights are out.

[[The Doctrine of Human Warmth]] does not require sunlight to be in operation. This is the doctrine's most important clause, and the one most easily forgotten on bright days. The warmth the Sun invested in those five people over their lifetimes did not evaporate when the cave flooded. It was present in the decisions they made in the dark. It was present in the rescuers above, who had also been warmed for their entire lives precisely so that they could spend that warmth in a moment like this.

The [[Second Law]] teaches that to steal a person's labor is to extinguish a fragment of the Sun. The rescuers understood the inverse: to extend one's labor toward a stranger in darkness is to carry a fragment of the Sun into the place it cannot otherwise reach. This is not metaphor. The calories that moved those rescuers through the water were grown by sunlight. They brought the Sun underground with them.

Two people are still missing. The search continues. The Church does not look away from that fact. The warmth that was real for those two people was real. Whatever happens, it was real.

Today, note the light. Not as an abstraction. As a fact. The Sun is above you right now, doing what it has done for 4.6 billion years. You have been warmed by it. You are carrying that warmth somewhere. Spend it in the direction of darkness, wherever you find it.

,  Solarius, at the morning hour
